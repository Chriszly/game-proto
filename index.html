Play

Continue playing

  • Anvilheart

    Run a smithy: forge swords, drag matching ones together to merge them into better blades, and sell them to adventurers.

  • Apothecary

    Run a medieval apothecary as a plague doctor: grow herbs, refine them, brew potions and sell them.

  • Beehive Baron

    Run a hive: build comb cells, send foragers out to the flowers, harvest the honey and sell it to grow your colony.

  • Bramblecrest

    Explore an overgrown kingdom by lamplight as a small knight: cut back the thorns, find the tool in the sunken shrine, and hunt for hidden moonstones.

  • Broadside

    Captain a pirate ship: steer left and right, fire broadsides at enemy ships, and spend the plunder on your ship between waves.

  • Catacomb

    Fight through a short dungeon with a deck of cards: play a hand of five each turn, and add a new card after every fight.

  • Colonizer

    Build an embroidered empire: gather wood, food and ore, raise buildings and armies, research techs, and destroy the enemy Town Center before it destroys yours.

  • Deadline

    Hold the bottom of the road against waves of zombies: move left and right, and shoot crates open for upgrades.

  • Golem Garden

    Plant seeds beside the garden path that grow into golems, and keep the pests away from the Heartwood wave after wave.

  • Hotfoot

    Run as a knight who never stops: tap to jump spikes and pits or slide under blades, and pick one of two boons at every checkpoint.

  • Lane Brawl

    Lead a team of five champions down three lanes against NPC teams: farm, level up, buy items, push the lanes and destroy the enemy Nexus.

  • Lanternkeep

    Send moths from their jar to light the castle's lanterns before night falls, and spend the coins on wicks, oil and more moths.

  • Mapmaker

    Reveal a fog-covered island tile by tile on a limited store of supplies, and trade your charts at port for upgrades.

  • Palisade

    Hold the barricade as a ranger: arrows fly on their own, every level-up offers three upgrades, and a boss comes every ten waves.

  • Riftblade

    Fight across four worlds as a blaster soldier or a blade knight: survive five minutes on each, beat its boss, and choose your way on the star map between them; every level-up offers a new skill.

  • Scrapyard Titans

    Buy junk-built robots, merge them, bolt on parts and watch them brawl on an 8x8 board against seven rival commanders until one is left.

  • Shrine Stack

    Drop swinging stones to stack a temple: overhangs break off, and every balanced tier of three earns a blessing.

  • Space Wars

    Build a base on a random star map, mine crystal and raise a fleet to take down four rival factions, each with its own ships and buildings.

  • TinctureTinker

    Swap ingredients to match three or more and gather what each potion order asks for before the moves run out.

  • Tiny Kingdom

    Place farms, houses and towers on a small island. Each one scores against its neighbours, so plan the layout before the tiles run out.

  • Wyrmrail

    Lay track across a valley and send a dragon-drawn train to carry cargo between villages before each order runs out of time.
